# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security vulnerabilities **privately** — not in public
issues or discussions.

Email **security@isonapse.com** with:

- a description of the issue and where it lives (component, version from
  `isonapse --version`, OS/arch),
- steps to reproduce, and
- your assessment of the impact.

You will get an acknowledgement within a few days. Please give us
reasonable time to ship a fix before disclosing publicly — we will credit
you in the release notes unless you prefer otherwise.

## Scope

Isonapse is itself a safety layer, so we take reports about ways to
**bypass or weaken it** especially seriously: policy evaluation being
sidestepped, the audit record being tampered with undetected, or secrets
handled by the product leaking. Reports about the beta being a beta
(rough edges, missing features) belong in ordinary issues.
